Unique Adaptation & Defense

Hypo-allergenic mucus coating prevents sea anemone stinging nematocysts from detonating, granting total symbiosis.

Ocean Ecological Impact

Defends host anemones from predatory butterflyfish while fertilizing tentacles with vital nitrogen nutrients.

Marine Curiosity

All clownfish are hatched as males; the dominant fish in a colony changes sex to become the reproductive queen.
